PRIVACY POLICY — OUR COMMUNITY COOKBOOK

Effective date: September 9, 2026
Last updated: September 9, 2026

Our Community Cookbook ("the app") is developed by Dexter's Lab ("we", "us").
This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else touches it, and how
to get rid of it. Contact: wdexterjackson@gmail.com


1. THE SHORT VERSION

You can install the app and cook from it without an account, without a
payment, and without giving us anything. Recipes you create while signed out
stay on your device.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with data
brokers. We do not run advertising. The app contains no analytics or
advertising SDK of any kind, and no third-party tracking.


2. WHAT WE COLLECT, AND WHEN

a) Nothing, until you make an account.
   Creating, editing, cooking, scaling, exporting, and printing your own
   recipes works with no account. That content is stored locally on your
   device and is not transmitted to us.

b) Account information (only if you sign in).
   Signing in is required only for cloud sync, Community Cookbooks, friends,
   and messaging. Depending on the method you choose we receive:
   - Sign in with Apple: the identifier Apple issues, and the email address
     Apple relays (which may be Apple's private relay address if you chose
     to hide your email).
   - Sign in with Google: your Google account identifier and email address.
   - Email and password: the email address you type. Passwords are handled
     by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
   We also store the display name you provide, so other members can see who
   contributed a recipe.

c) Content you create.
   Recipes, ingredients, steps, notes, photos you attach, cookbooks and
   chapters, meal plans, and grocery lists. If you sign in, this syncs to
   your account so it survives a lost device.

d) Community content.
   If you join or create a Community Cookbook: your membership, your role,
   recipes you publish there, comments and likes you leave, and join
   requests or invitations you send or accept.

e) Messages.
   Direct messages, shared recipes, and shared meal plans you send to
   friends inside the app.

f) An optional, self-typed location.
   Settings lets you enter a city and state (or city and country) so recipe
   attribution can say where a recipe came from. This is text you type. The
   app does not use GPS, does not include any location framework, and never
   reads your device's location.

g) Purchases.
   Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment card,
   billing address, or Apple ID password. We store only the resulting
   entitlement — whether your account has Community Access, cookbook
   credits, or an active Annual Community Plus membership — together with
   the transaction identifier Apple provides so renewals and refunds can be
   matched to the right account.

h) A small amount of app-preference data.
   For example whether you have hidden the Featured Cookbooks shelf or the
   Getting Started section, and when you last viewed comment activity, so
   your choices follow your account across devices.


3. WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

- No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
- No analytics, behavioral profiling, or usage tracking SDK.
- No contact list, calendar, microphone, or health data.
- No device location.
- No biometric data.
- No browsing activity outside the app.


4. CAMERA AND PHOTOS

The app asks for camera access to scan a QR code (joining a community,
adding a friend, or signing in on Apple TV) and to photograph a recipe card.
It asks for photo library access only when you choose a photo to attach.

Recipe photo scanning runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision
framework. A photo you scan for text is not uploaded to us or to any third
party for that purpose. If you then attach the photo to a recipe and you are
signed in, the photo syncs with that recipe like any other content.


5. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS USED

Solely to operate the app: to authenticate you, to store and sync your
content, to show community members who wrote a recipe, to deliver messages
you send, to enforce community permissions and moderation, to apply
purchases to the correct account, and to respond to support requests.

We do not use your content to train machine-learning models, and we do not
sell or rent it.


6. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND THIRD PARTIES

- Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud
  Functions) hosts the app's accounts, data, and photos, and runs its
  server-side logic. Google processes this data on our behalf.
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Google Sign-In, if you choose that sign-in method.
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Apple, for Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, subscription status,
  and (with your permission) push notifications.
  https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Spoonacular and TheMealDB provide the optional recipe Discover search. A
  search you type there is sent to those services to return results. Do not
  type personal information into that search field.
  https://spoonacular.com/food-api/terms  |  https://www.themealdb.com
- YouTube. If a recipe includes a video link, the video plays through
  YouTube's official player, and YouTube may set cookies and collect data
  under Google's policy while it plays.
  https://policies.google.com/privacy

We disclose information otherwise only when legally required, or to protect
the rights and safety of users.


7. WHAT OTHER PEOPLE CAN SEE

- Your personal cookbook is private to your account.
- A recipe you publish to a Community Cookbook is visible to that
  community's members, along with your display name.
- Community administrators can see the membership roster and moderate
  published recipes. Ordinary members cannot see the roster.
- Recipe attribution is durable by design: when someone copies a community
  recipe, the copy permanently records the original author and community.
  Consider this before publishing under a name you would not want kept.
- Email discoverability is optional. Settings has a switch controlling
  whether friends can find you by email address; turning it off stops that
  lookup from matching you.


8. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Your content is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

Deleting your account from within the app (Account > Delete Account) removes
your profile, public profile, entitlements, memberships, friendships and
friend requests, direct messages, shared recipes, comments, cloud-synced
cookbooks and recipes, published community recipes, and your uploaded photos.
Communities where you are the last remaining member are deleted with you.

Two limits to be aware of, stated plainly:
- If you are the sole administrator of a community that still has other
  members, deletion is blocked until you promote another administrator or
  remove the community. This exists so a community is never orphaned.
- Content that other people have already copied into their own cookbooks
  belongs to them and remains in their cookbooks, carrying the attribution
  recorded at the time of copying.

Recipes stored only on your device are removed when you delete the app.

If a lapsed Annual Community Plus membership is not renewed, photos stored
under that membership may be removed after a grace period; the app warns
before this happens.


9. YOUR RIGHTS

You may access, correct, export, or delete your information at any time:
- Access and correction: directly in the app.
- Export: any cookbook can be exported as a PDF, and recipes can be shared
  as files, from within the app.
- Deletion: Account > Delete Account, as described above.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also
have the right to object to or restrict processing, the right to data
portability, and the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.
Our legal basis for processing is performance of our agreement with you
(operating the app) and your consent where you have given it.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal
information is collected, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale. We do
not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will
not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To make a request, email wdexterjackson@gmail.com. We may need to verify
that you control the account in question.


10. CHILDREN

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created
an account, email us and we will delete it. Community Cookbooks are
invitation- or approval-based; an adult administrator controls who joins and
what is published.


11. DATA SECURITY AND LOCATION

Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and protected by server-side
security rules that restrict each record to the accounts entitled to it.
Passwords are hashed and managed by Firebase Authentication. Entitlement
grants are verified server-side and cannot be granted by a client.

Data may be stored and processed in the United States and other countries
where Google operates. No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.


12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and post the
revised version at the same address. Material changes will be surfaced in
the app. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept it.
