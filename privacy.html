Ask Lellon — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026 · Last updated: September 6, 2026

Ask Lellon (“the app”) helps you keep track of your medications, supplements, doses and refills, and flags possible interactions. This policy explains what the app collects, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and the control you have over it.

The short version. By default, everything you enter stays on your device. Cloud storage is opt-in. The app has no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDK, and nothing about you or your health is ever sold, rented, or shared for marketing. Pill photos are analyzed on your device and never uploaded or saved.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Ask Lellon is published by Dexter’s Lab. For any privacy question, request, or complaint, contact WDexterJackson@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the information described below.

2. Information the app holds

Information you enter

  • Health profile — your name, date of birth, gender, pregnancy status, allergies, medical conditions, and an emergency contact name and phone number. All of it is optional; the app works with any subset of it filled in.
  • Medicine cabinet — the medications and supplements you add, their dosage, form, directions, schedule, start and end dates, refill settings, and reminder preferences.
  • Dose history — the record of doses you mark as taken, skipped, or missed, with their timestamps.

Account information

You can use Ask Lellon without an account. If you choose to sign in — with Sign in with Apple or with Google — we receive the account identifier and email address the provider returns, and store an internal user ID. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

Information generated by the app

  • Push notification token — a device token issued by Apple and handled through Firebase Cloud Messaging, used to deliver dose reminders and caregiver alerts. It identifies a device installation, not you personally.
  • Issue reports — if you use “Report an Issue,” the app sends the report type, title, and description you wrote, plus your app version, iOS version, and device model, so the problem can be reproduced.

3. Where your data is stored

Ask Lellon has two storage modes, and you choose which one applies.

  • On-device storage (the default). Your profile, medicine cabinet, and dose history are stored only in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. They are not transmitted to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, that data is gone.
  • Cloud storage (opt-in). If you turn on cloud storage in Settings, the same data is mirrored to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account so it survives a lost device and syncs across your devices. Cloud storage is also enabled automatically if you choose to share your dose history with a connection, because sharing cannot work without it — you are told this at the point you make that choice.

Cloud data is protected by security rules that limit each document to the account that owns it and, where you have granted it, the connections you have approved. Firebase encrypts data in transit and at rest.

4. Sharing with caregivers and connections

You may invite another person as a connection and grant them visibility into your dose history — for example a family member helping you stay on schedule. This is entirely opt-in and specific to the person you invite. Depending on what you enable, a connection may receive missed-dose alerts, a daily summary of your adherence, and the ability to send you a reminder nudge. You can revoke a connection at any time in the app, which stops all further sharing.

Price comparison links

The app can link out to third-party prescription price sites such as GoodRx, RxSaver, SingleCare, and WellRx. Following one of these links opens your browser and leaves Ask Lellon; from that point the destination site’s own privacy policy applies, and we have no control over and no visibility into what it collects. Small site icons shown next to these links are loaded from Google’s public favicon service.

6. What the app does not do

  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers.
  • No analytics, behavioral tracking, or cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No sale of personal information, and no sharing of it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • No use of your health information for any purpose other than providing the features you asked for.
  • No upload or retention of pill photographs.

7. How long data is kept

On-device data lasts until you delete the entry or delete the app. Cloud data lasts until you delete it or delete your account. Issue reports are retained in our support records for as long as needed to resolve the issue and keep a history of reported problems.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account. Sign-in is required only for cloud sync and caregiver sharing.
  • Stay local. On-device storage is the default and can be kept indefinitely.
  • Delete your account. Settings contains a Delete Account action that removes your account and the cloud data associated with it.
  • Revoke sharing. Remove a connection at any time to stop all sharing with that person.
  • Revoke permissions. Camera, photos, location, Health, and notification access can be withdrawn at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Access, correction, portability, and erasure. Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR and UK GDPR, and under California law (CCPA/CPRA) — you may have the right to request a copy of your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Write to WDexterJackson@gmail.com and we will respond within the period the applicable law requires.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are your consent (for health data, notifications, and optional device permissions), performance of a contract (operating the app and your account), and our legitimate interests (keeping the app secure and fixing reported problems). You may withdraw consent at any time.

9. Children

Ask Lellon is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it. A parent or guardian may of course use the app to manage a child’s medications; in that case the account belongs to the adult.

10. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Cloud data is stored in Google Firebase with access rules that scope each record to its owner and their approved connections. Credentials held on your device are kept in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify affected users of a breach as required by law.

11. International transfers

Our service providers, including Google Firebase and SendGrid, operate in the United States and may process data there. If you use the app from outside the United States, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in a country whose data protection laws differ from those of your own.

12. Not medical advice

Ask Lellon is a personal tracking and reference tool, not a medical device or a healthcare provider, and the information it shows — including interaction warnings drawn from public drug databases — is not medical advice and is not a substitute for a licensed pharmacist or physician. We are not a HIPAA covered entity, and the health information you enter is governed by this policy rather than by HIPAA. Always consult a qualified professional before changing how you take a medication.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app. Continuing to use Ask Lellon after a change means you accept the updated policy.
